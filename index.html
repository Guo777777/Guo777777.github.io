Research

What I work on

My work sits at the intersection of medical imaging, computer vision, and multimodal language models, with image quality at the center.

Biomedical Image Analysis
Segmentation, landmark detection, and quantitative measurement across CT, CBCT, X-ray, and echocardiography.
Computer Vision
Low-level vision and image restoration, plus segmentation-difficulty estimation.
Multimodal LLMs · VLM / MLLM
Vision-language models as priors and evaluators for medical imaging tasks.
Image Quality Assessment
Quality-aware restoration and model design guided by perceptual priors.

Hyperspherical feature geometry, a current research direction: representing visual attributes as directions on a unit sphere.
