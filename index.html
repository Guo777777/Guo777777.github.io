Duke BME · VIP Lab Durham, North Carolina

Xinge Guo

M.S. in Biomedical Engineering
Vision & Image Processing Lab · advised by Prof. Sina Farsiu

I work at the intersection of biomedical image analysis, computer vision, and multimodal large language models, with a focus on image quality assessment and using vision-language priors to make medical imaging systems more robust and generalizable. Before Duke I earned my B.E. (Class of Excellence) from Shenzhen University, building deep models for echocardiogram landmark detection, CBCT orthodontic analysis, and semi-supervised tooth segmentation.
